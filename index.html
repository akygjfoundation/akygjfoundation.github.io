About Us

Gram Jyoti Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to rural development, education, health, and livelihood empowerment.

We work closely with village communities to create sustainable solutions that improve quality of life and promote self-reliance.

Our Mission

To uplift rural lives by helping people who are in distress, conducting cultural events to maintain good comradeship, and building a village amusement infrastructure.

Our Vision

To create hope for the people who are in distress and giving back to society.

Our Focus Areas

📘

Education

Supporting quality education and learning opportunities for rural children.

🏥

Health & Hygiene

Promoting basic healthcare, cleanliness, and healthy living practices.

🌱

Rural Development

Encouraging sustainable agriculture and village development programs.

🤝

Empowering communities through awareness, training, and support.
